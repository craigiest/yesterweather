Daily highs, lows, precipitation, normals (1991–2020) and records come from NOAA station data via ACIS. Hourly readings come from the nearest National Weather Service station. Where NOAA has no data (outside the US, or not yet reported), values come from Open-Meteo model data. Air quality is the US EPA AQI from Open-Meteo.
